A steady path has none of {{ p.symbol }}'s swings: on it every call decays, so the take-profits all fill, and the plan doesn't add up the premiums, which would be a best case. On real prices some calls end in the money, rolls come sooner or later, and the dates move: the plan is the order of things, and about when. The rules: a take-profit at 25%, a roll up a rung at 97% of the strike to the shortest expiry four to six weeks on, no call over a report (reports past the next are taken to be a quarter apart), and each new call at the lowest rung above the price and its roll zone, never below the rung reached, if it pays 1% of the price.
