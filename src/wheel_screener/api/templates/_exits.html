Value is the money that changes hands — earned on a short position, paid on a long one — and the rate is that over the capital it commits, annualised — the same unit for every row, so the top one wins. Where a row also shows a cash figure, the gap is intrinsic being sold: money that arrives now and is handed back at expiry. A roll is scored over the days it adds — the second figure in its Days cell — since the days before your current expiry are yours either way.
